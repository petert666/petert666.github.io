ABOUT

About Me

I am enrolled in the integrated M.S.–Ph.D. program in Control Science and Engineering at the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China and expect to graduate in July 2027. My research covers extreme-climate impact modeling, critical component identification, cross-scale vulnerability analysis, cascading-failure propagation assessment, and multi-energy system optimization.

I conducted visiting doctoral research at the Department of Energy, Politecnico di Torino. I have experience in modeling, optimization, and simulation using MATLAB, YALMIP, CPLEX, Python, PyTorch, and MATPOWER.

RESEARCH

Research Interests

01

Power System Resilience

Resilience assessment, resilient scheduling, and secure power supply under extreme heat, disaster disturbances, and source–load uncertainty.

02

Vulnerability Analysis

Identification of critical buses, branches, and skeleton structures by integrating complex-network theory with power-flow mechanisms and operating-state risk.

03

Optimization and Scheduling

Multi-timescale coordinated scheduling of wind, solar, hydro, thermal, and storage resources, including demand response and coordinated storage regulation.

04

Distribution Network Analysis

Fault diagnosis, situational awareness, fault location, and critical-component risk assessment in distribution networks with high renewable penetration.

Ongoing

Higher-Order Vulnerability Assessment of Transmission Lines via Contextual Propagation Strength Learning

Construct propagation labels using cascading-failure simulation and counterfactual power-flow recalculation, and develop a dual-channel Graph Transformer to learn background risk and conditional propagation strength between transmission lines.

MATLABPythonPyTorch
Oct. 2025 – Jan. 2026
Second-Round Review

Vulnerability-Oriented Power System Renormalization for Critical Skeleton Network Analysis

Construct a composite diffusion metric from electrical coupling, power-flow stress, and post-contingency flow redistribution, and combine spectral partitioning, skeleton extraction, and Ward equivalencing to preserve vulnerable branches across scales.

Hit rate for the top 10% vulnerable branches: 94.74%–100%
Feb. 2025 – Sep. 2025
Published

Resilience-Oriented Scheduling of Wind–Solar–Hydro–Thermal–Storage Multi-Energy Complementary Systems under Extreme Heat

Model temperature-dependent generation efficiency, line and transformer thermal ratings, and temperature-sensitive demand, and develop a multi-timescale scheduling framework with demand response and graded load protection.
